## 🚀 Overview
Filmora for Windows is a desktop video editor from Wondershare designed for creators who want a short learning curve without giving up modern editing tools. It combines a drag-and-drop timeline, a large library of effects, transitions, and titles, and built-in audio tools so you can take footage from raw clips to a finished, share-ready video. Whether you are assembling a YouTube vlog, a short social clip, a family memory, or a workplace presentation, the Wondershare Filmora video editor offers a clear interface, royalty-free assets, and one-click export presets for the most popular destinations.

## 🎯 Getting Started
1. Open Filmora and create a new project, choosing your target resolution and frame rate (for example 1920×1080 at 30 fps).
2. Drag your video, photo, and audio files into the Media panel, then onto the timeline in the order you want.
3. Trim and split clips on the timeline, then add transitions, titles, and effects from the side panel.
4. Open the Audio tab to fine-tune levels, fade in or out, and drop in background music from the built-in library.
5. Click **Export**, pick a preset (MP4, YouTube, or social) and render your finished video.
